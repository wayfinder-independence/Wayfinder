# Vision

Many of the hardest parts of a day are small moments. What is happening today? What should I wear? What do I need? What comes next?

For many people these answers come without thinking. For many neurodivergent people they can bring stress, uncertainty, and a need to rely on someone else.

Wayfinder's long-term vision is a world where more neurodivergent people can move through everyday life with confidence and say, "I can do this myself."

Over time, that could mean help with understanding a schedule, getting ready for the day, choosing what to wear, keeping up with medication, cooking, grooming, exercise, and other everyday life skills.

Independence does not mean doing everything alone. It means having what you need to make your own choices. Wayfinder should support the person, respect the people who care for them, and never replace either.
