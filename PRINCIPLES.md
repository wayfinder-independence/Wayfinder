# Principles

1. **The person decides.** Wayfinder helps people make their own choices. It does not choose for them, watch them, or control them.

2. **Real days matter most.** Something is only useful if it helps in a person's real life.

3. **Keep it simple and calm.** Clear words, few steps, nothing confusing.

4. **Respect privacy.** Ask only for what is needed, and never share a person's information without their permission.

5. **Be honest when unsure.** If Wayfinder is not certain, it says so and asks instead of guessing.
